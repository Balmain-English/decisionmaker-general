🦁 African Safari: Pre-Safari Briefing

Pre-Expedition Speaking

Part 1: Pre-Safari Discussion Questions

Discuss these four questions with a partner or small group before heading out into the reserve.

Question 1 • Wildlife Travel

Have you ever seen wild animals in their natural habitat or visited a national park? What was the most memorable part?

Question 2 • Tour Guide Qualities

What skills and personality traits make a safari ranger or tour guide truly professional when leading international guests?

Question 3 • Wildlife Safety Rules

Why is it so important for visitors to follow strict safety rules—such as staying quiet or remaining inside the vehicle—near wild animals?

Question 4 • Ethical Tourism

How can travelers support local communities and protect fragile ecosystems responsibly when visiting another country?

💬 Useful Speaking Phrases

  • "In my opinion, a great ranger should..."
  • "I've always wanted to see... because..."
  • "One essential safety rule is to... so that..."
  • "Responsible tourists can help local people by..."
