🏸 Pre-Tournament Briefing & Warm-Up

Part 1 • Pair / Group Speaking Warm-Up

Pre-Tournament Discussion Questions

Discuss these four questions with a partner or small group before you organize the Green Valley tournament.

Question 1 • Sports Experience

Have you ever played badminton or watched a local sports tournament? What made the event fun or exciting?

Question 2 • Event Planning

What are the three most important things an organizer needs to prepare before a sports competition begins?

Question 3 • Indoor vs. Outdoor

Do you prefer playing racket sports indoors in a sports hall or outdoors in a park? Why?

Question 4 • Crisis Management

Imagine the lights go out or equipment breaks in the middle of a final match. How should a good manager react?

🗣️ Useful Speaking Phrases

  • "In my opinion, the most important thing is..."
  • "One advantage of playing indoors is..."
  • "If we have a small budget, we should..."
  • "To keep players safe and happy, a manager needs to..."
