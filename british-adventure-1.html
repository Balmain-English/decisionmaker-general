Part 1 • Speaking Warm-Up

Pre-Departure Discussion Questions

Discuss these four A2 travel questions with a partner or in small groups before Max sets off for Britain.

1 • Choosing a UK Destination

If you could visit London, Edinburgh, Manchester, or Cornwall, which place would you choose first and why?

2 • Changeable British Weather

British weather can change from sunshine to rain in ten minutes! What clothes and items do you always pack when you travel?

3 • Polite Travel English

Why are polite phrases like "Excuse me" and "Could I please..." so important when buying tickets or ordering food in the UK?

4 • Handling Travel Mix-Ups

Have you ever got lost in a new city or had a small problem on a train or at a hotel? How did you ask for help?

💬 Useful Speaking Phrases

  • "I would like to visit... because..."
  • "In my suitcase, I always pack... so I stay dry."
  • "In the UK, it is polite to say 'Excuse me' when..."
  • "If I get lost or have a problem, I usually ask..."
