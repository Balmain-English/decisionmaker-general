Section A • Pre-Game Speaking Warm-Up

Pre-Game Discussion Questions

Discuss these four questions with a partner or class before starting the survival challenge.

Question 1 • Personal Experience

What is the coldest weather or winter storm you have ever experienced, and how did people prepare for it?

Question 2 • Emergency Gear

If a sudden blizzard trapped you at home or inside your car, what five emergency items would be most essential to have?

Question 3 • Cold-Weather Myths

Why do people often believe myths like drinking alcohol to warm up or rubbing frostbitten skin with snow, and why are these dangerous?

Question 4 • Winter Power Outage

Imagine the electricity and central heating fail during a sub-zero night. What steps would you take to keep your home and family warm?

💬 Useful Speaking Phrases

  • "A common misconception about cold weather is..."
  • "If the heating went out overnight, I would immediately..."
  • "To prevent heat loss, it is much safer to..."
