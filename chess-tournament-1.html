Pre-Tournament Briefing: Warm-Up & Vocabulary

Part 1 • Speaking Warm-Up

Discussion Questions

Discuss these four questions with a partner or group before opening the Old Library doors to players.

Question 1

Have you ever played chess or another strategy board game? What skills do you think a strong player needs most?

Question 2

Why are silence and concentration so important during a serious match or an important exam?

Question 3

Imagine you are organizing a public event inside a quiet library. What rules would you set for visitors and players?

Question 4

How should a fair organizer or referee handle a disagreement between two competitive players?

💬 Useful Speaking Phrases

  • "In my opinion, the most essential factor is..."
  • "To keep the atmosphere fair, an organizer should..."
  • "If a disturbance happens mid-game, I would..."
  • "On the one hand..., on the other hand..."
