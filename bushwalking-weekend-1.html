⛰️ 1. Blue Mountains National Park

Sandstone Canyons & Mist

🗿 The Three Sisters 🪜 Giant Stairway 🌿 Grand Canyon Track 💦 Wentworth Falls 🏰 Ruined Castle

🌊 2. Royal National Park (The Coast Track)

Sea Cliffs & Wave Platforms

Trek 26km along dramatic Tasman Sea sandstone cliffs from Bundeena to Otford, crossing tidal lagoons and subtropical palm jungle.

🍰 Wedding Cake Rock 🎱 Figure Eight Pools 🏖️ Wattamolla Lagoon 🦅 Eagle Rock & Curracurrong 🌴 Palm Jungle
⚠️ Key Hazards: Rogue ocean waves on rock shelves, Southerly Buster cliff gales, zero-shade UV exposure & paralysis ticks.

🏔️ 3. Kosciuszko National Park (Main Range)

Alpine Summits & Glacial Lakes

Traverse Australia's highest alpine plateau above the treeline from Charlotte Pass, passing glacial cirques and historic stone huts.

🇦🇺 Mt Kosciuszko (2,228m) 💎 Blue Lake Cirque 🏠 Seaman's Hut ❄️ Snowy River Headwaters 🌳 Twisted Snow Gums
⚠️ Key Hazards: Sudden summer blizzards & whiteouts, hypothermia, icy river fords, high-altitude UV & fragile alpine flora.
