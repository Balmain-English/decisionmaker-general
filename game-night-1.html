The Casual Game Night 🍕

Welcome to The Casual Game Night! This is an interactive scenario for adult English learners. Your good friend, Alex, has invited you over for a relaxed evening of pizza and board games with a few other friends. You want to have a good time and be a great guest, but small social challenges keep popping up!

Game Rules & Points:
  • 10 Scenarios: Make the best social choice for hanging out with friends.
  • 4 Options Every Time: The answers appear in a random order every game.
  • 🟩 Excellent Answer (+2 points): Friendly, engaging, and uses great natural English!
  • 🟦 Good Answer (+1 point): A perfectly fine and polite choice.
  • 🟧 Average Answer (+0 points): Okay, but maybe a bit quiet or slightly awkward.
  • 🟥 Bad Answer (-1 point): Rude, aggressive, or completely kills the fun vibe. You lose 1 point and must try again!
  • Maximum Points: 20 Points.
